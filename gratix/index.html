What is Gratuity Calculator? Complete Guide 2026

Gratuity is a lump sum amount paid by employer to employee as a token of thanks for continuous 5 years of service. It is governed by Payment of Gratuity Act 1972. Our Gratix Gratuity Calculator calculates exact gratuity as per government formula: Gratuity = (15 × Last Drawn Basic Salary + DA × Years of Service) / 26. The 15 days salary for each year, 26 days = working days in month (excluding 4 Sundays). Our calculator is 100% free, unlimited, no watermark, accurate as per latest 2026 rules where tax-free limit is Rs 20 Lakh.

For example, if your last basic + DA is Rs 60,000 and you worked for 7 years, gratuity = (15*60000*7)/26 = Rs 2,42,307. This is tax-free if total gratuity in lifetime is under Rs 20L. If you work 20 years with Rs 1L basic, gratuity = (15*100000*20)/26 = Rs 11,53,846 tax-free. If gratuity exceeds 20L, excess is taxable as per slab.

How Gratuity Calculator Works? Formula Explained

Govt Formula for companies covered under Gratuity Act (10+ employees): Gratuity = (15/26) × Last Basic + DA × Years. 15 = 15 days salary per year. 26 = working days per month. Years: If you worked 7 years 6 months, it counts as 8 years (if >6 months, round to next year). If 7 years 4 months, counts as 7 years. Our calculator auto handles this with months dropdown.

For companies NOT covered under Gratuity Act (<10 employees): Formula = (15/30) × Last Basic × Years. Here 30 days taken, not 26, so gratuity slightly less: (15*60000*7)/30 = Rs 2,10,000 vs Rs 2,42,307 for covered. Most private companies are covered, so we default to 15/26.

Gratuity Rules You Must Know 2026

1. Eligibility: Minimum 4 years 10 months 11 days continuous service (Supreme Court ruling). Not exactly 5 years. 2. Tax-Free Limit: Rs 20 Lakh lifetime tax-free (increased from 10L in 2018). Govt employees have no limit, full tax-free. 3. Maximum: No maximum limit for private, but tax-free only till 20L. 4. When Paid: Within 30 days of leaving, else employer pays 10% interest. 5. Nominee: Must fill Form F for nominee. 6. Forfeiture: Gratuity can be forfeited only if employee does fraud, riot, violence.

How to Increase Gratuity Amount?

Gratuity depends on last basic, not average. So negotiate higher basic (not special allowance) at time of joining. Keep basic 40-50% of CTC, not 30%. Don't leave before 4Y 10M - you lose full gratuity. If possible, complete 6 months extra to get extra year count. For example, 4Y 7M = 5Y gratuity. Ask HR for Form I for gratuity claim. If company delays >30 days, you can complain to Labour Commissioner.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1. How much gratuity after 5 years?
Formula: (15*Basic*5)/26. For Rs 50k basic, gratuity after 5 years = Rs 1,44,230 tax-free.
Q2. Is gratuity taxable?
Gratuity up to Rs 20 Lakh lifetime is tax-free for private employees. Govt employees full exempt. Above 20L taxable as per slab.
Q3. Am I eligible for gratuity after 4 years?
Yes, as per Supreme Court, 4 years 10 months 11 days continuous service is considered 5 years, so eligible.
Q4. What is last drawn salary for gratuity?
Last drawn Basic + Dearness Allowance (DA) at time of leaving, not gross, not CTC, not average.
