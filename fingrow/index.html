FD Calculator Formula - How Banks Calculate Interest?

Banks in India use quarterly compounding formula: Maturity = P * (1 + r/4)^(4*n) where P is principal, r is annual rate/100, n is years. For example, Rs 5 Lakh at 7.5% for 5 years: Maturity = 500000*(1+0.075/4)^(20) = Rs 7,24,974 approx. Our calculator uses same formula with daily update. If you choose 7.5% for 5 years, you get Rs 2,17,506 interest which is 43% extra on principal. This is power of compounding quarterly, not yearly.

Many users search for FD calculator monthly interest payout vs cumulative. Monthly payout FD gives you interest every month in your savings account, but maturity is less. Cumulative FD gives you full maturity at end, higher returns. Our calculator shows cumulative by default, which is best for wealth creation. If you want monthly income, divide total interest by months shown in monthly interest box.

RD Calculator Formula - Monthly Compounding Magic

RD formula is slightly different: Maturity = M * [((1 + r/4)^(4*n) -1) / (1 - (1 + r/4)^(-1/3)) ] where M is monthly installment. Simplified for users, if you invest Rs 10,000 monthly for 5 years at 7.5%, total investment is Rs 6,00,000, interest is Rs 1,20,500, maturity Rs 7,20,500. RD is best for creating discipline, you cannot miss monthly installment else penalty.

RD is ideal for students, housewives, and freelancers who get monthly income. You can start RD with just Rs 500 per month in Post Office. SBI RD rate is 6.5% to 7% for 5 years, HDFC gives 7.1%, Post Office RD gives 6.7% currently. Our calculator lets you compare all banks instantly by changing rate slider.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is current SBI FD rate 2026?
SBI FD rate is 6.5% to 7.5% for general public, 7% to 8% for senior citizens for 5 years tenure.
Q2. Is RD better than FD?
RD is better for monthly income earners, FD is better for lump sum. Return wise both same if rate same, but FD gives slightly higher due to full principal compounding from day 1.
Q3. Is FD interest taxable?
Yes, FD interest is taxable as per slab, TDS 10% if interest > Rs 40,000 per year (Rs 50,000 for senior citizen). RD interest also taxable.
