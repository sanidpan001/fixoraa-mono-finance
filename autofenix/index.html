What is Car Loan EMI Calculator with Down Payment?

When you buy a car/bike, you don't pay full price. You pay some down payment (e.g., 20%) and bank pays rest as loan. You return bank money in EMI. Formula: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]. Here P is loan amount (Price - Down Payment). Our tool does reverse calculation too: you enter vehicle price and down payment %, it automatically calculates loan amount. This is the real-world calculation that SBI, HDFC, ICICI use.
