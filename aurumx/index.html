How Gold Calculator Works? Formula

Gold SIP Formula: Future Value = Monthly Investment * [((1+r/12)^(n*12)-1)/(r/12)]*(1+r/12) where r is expected annual gold price growth. Gold Lump Sum: Future Value = Investment * (1+r)^n * (Future Gold Price / Current Gold Price). We also show grams of gold you will own.

Example: Monthly Rs 10,000 Gold SIP for 10 years at 11% growth: Total invested Rs 12,00,000. Future value Rs 21,89,873. Gain Rs 9,89,873 (about 82% return). Gold bought: If gold price today Rs 1,48,000/10g = Rs 14,800/g, monthly 10k buys about 0.68g per month. At today's price your total investment buys about 81.1g of gold, and its future value is higher because gold price will be ~Rs 4,20,234/10g after 10 years at 11% growth.

Gold SIP vs Gold Lump Sum vs Jewellery - Which is Better?

Gold SIP (Monthly): Best for salaried people, averages price, no timing risk, disciplined. Gold Lump Sum: Best if you have large cash and gold price just corrected 10-15%.
