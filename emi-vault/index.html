What is Home Loan EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is the fixed amount you pay to the bank every month. EMI has two parts: Principal (actual loan amount) and Interest. In early years, your EMI has more interest part, and later it has more principal part. Formula used is: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1] where P is principal, R is monthly interest rate, N is number of months.

What is Prepayment and Why is it Powerful?

Prepayment means paying extra money over your normal EMI. There are two types we have added in this tool: 1. Monthly Prepayment (e.g., you pay ₹5000 extra every month), 2. One-time Prepayment (e.g., you got a bonus of ₹2 Lakh and you pay it once). Prepayment directly reduces your principal amount, which reduces your interest drastically. For example, if you take a ₹50 Lakh loan for 20 years at 8.5%, your total interest is ₹54 Lakh. If you just pay ₹5000 extra per month, you will save nearly ₹12 Lakh in interest and your loan will finish 3 years early! This is the secret of rich people.
